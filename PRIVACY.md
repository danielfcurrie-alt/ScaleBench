# ScaleBench Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

ScaleBench is a local Bluetooth scale testing and analysis app. It does not use accounts, advertising, third-party analytics, or cloud sync.

## Data ScaleBench Handles

ScaleBench may store the following data locally on your device:

- Bluetooth scale names, identifiers, advertised services, and protocol details.
- Raw Bluetooth packets received from supported scales.
- Parsed weight samples, timing data, battery telemetry, and diagnostic classifications.
- Saved recordings, notes you enter, imported JSON recordings, exported JSON files, and scorecard images.
- Device Utility reports, including connected-device capability details and detected USB serial devices where supported.

## How Data Is Used

ScaleBench uses this data to:

- Connect to supported scales.
- Record and analyze scale behavior.
- Calculate ScaleBench Standard v1 results and diagnostics.
- Save, import, export, and compare recordings.
- Generate device reports and scorecards when you choose to do so.

## Sharing and Export

ScaleBench does not automatically send recordings, packets, notes, diagnostics, or device reports to the developer or any third party.

Data leaves your device only when you choose to export, share, copy, back up, or otherwise transfer it using system sharing, file pickers, GitHub, email, cloud storage, or another app.

## Third-Party Services

ScaleBench does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

Android builds may include Nordic Semiconductor Bluetooth/DFU libraries to support Bluetooth communication and compatible device firmware update workflows. Those libraries run in the app to communicate with nearby devices and do not create a ScaleBench cloud account or ScaleBench analytics service.

## Permissions

ScaleBench may request Bluetooth, nearby-device, location, USB, file, or document access permissions depending on platform and feature. These permissions are used for scanning, connecting, recording, importing, exporting, and device utility workflows.

## Retention and Deletion

Saved recordings and notes remain on your device until you delete them, uninstall the app, clear app data, or remove exported files yourself.

## Children

ScaleBench is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as ScaleBench changes. Material changes should be reflected in this file and in the app release notes.
